How to play
  • Belt: every gun hangs on your belt. Reach down and hold grip to draw one, let go to put it back.
  • Two hands: rifles, the MP9, the Nova and the AWP only fire with your other hand gripping the front.
  • Scope: raise the AWP until the scope is right in front of your eye.
  • Reload: grip the mag with your other hand and pull it out (or press B/Y to drop it), grab a fresh one from the pouch on the front of your belt, and push it into the gun. The Nova takes shells one at a time.
  • Move: left stick (right stick if you're left-handed), turn with the other stick. Hold A/X to plant or defuse.
