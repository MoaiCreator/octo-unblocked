How to play

  • T: carry the bomb to site A or B and hold E to plant. Stop the CTs from defusing it.
  • CT: eliminate the Ts or defuse the bomb (a kit halves defuse time).
  • First to 7 rounds wins. Sides swap after round 6. Win rounds to earn money and buy better gear.
  • Online: one player hosts and shares the room code; everyone else joins. Empty slots are filled with bots.

Settings

Desktop browser with keyboard and mouse recommended. Multiplayer pairs players through PeerJS' free public broker, then connects them directly.
